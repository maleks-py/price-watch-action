# Product photo credits

Filenames match product ids in `products.json`. The Action ignores this file
(only `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, and `.webp` are processed).

Every image currently in this folder was **AI-generated** as a studio catalog
shot: one subject, white background, no text, no watermark. They are not
photographs taken by the user, and they are not stock downloads. No
third-party stock license applies. Do not treat them as photos of a specific
real animal or cut.

Stock CDNs (Pexels, Unsplash, Pixabay, Wikimedia file hosts) were blocked from
this environment. Web-search thumbnails were rejected: several were Dreamstime,
Vecteezy, Getty, or Unsplash+ previews with watermarks or paid licenses.
